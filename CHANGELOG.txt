   later): the kernel lists Export in its "Which import or export tool do I need?" page, the other tools' "Looking
   for…?" boxes, the "Import and export" lists, the class page ("Export the objects of this class…") and the search

   export" list to every left menu of the module; on an older kernel both are left out

   opens in Import with its column manifest, as if it had been uploaded, for the user who may see the job and use

   XrowExtractSchema never saw xrowextract_schedule, _destination and _history, tried to create them again on
   every write and failed with "table already exists" (schedules could not be saved). The tables are now looked
   up in sqlite_master (information_schema on PostgreSQL)
-  Fixed the Export tab's icon: it shows upload (Icon=upload in [Topmenu_xrowextract] of menu.ini), as data goes
   out of the site; Imports, which brings data in, keeps download. 2.5.19 had them the wrong way round
